Privacy

· 2026-10-03

Scope

This notice covers the current Daily Planner build. App Store publication and subscription-service setup are pending; purchase-related processing described below applies when those services are enabled.

Who to contact

Daily Planner is developed by SLYMN. Contact: smanvar4255@gmail.com.

Tasks, notes and photos

The app stores task records, journal text, completion history, preferences and photos you select in its local storage. The current build has no app account, advertising SDK or AI journal-writing service. Device data is used to provide planning, journal and search features.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice entry is optional. The app asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission when you use it. Audio is used to transcribe speech into editable text. Depending on your device and Apple service support, recognition may send audio to Apple and require a network connection. The initial scope does not include keeping a separate audio-recording library.

iCloud status

iCloud synchronisation is not available in the current build and is not included in the current Pro feature list. This notice will be updated before any future cloud synchronisation is enabled.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles purchases. RevenueCat is planned for subscription validation and entitlement management. Its SDK may process an app user identifier, purchase history, entitlement and transaction data, and technical metadata. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. The final SDK configuration and data declarations will be verified before release.

Notifications and permissions

Local reminders use your task dates and reminder choices. Notifications can be disabled in iOS Settings. Photo access uses your selected items. Refusing optional microphone, speech or notification permissions does not require sending journal data to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Local content is kept until you remove it or the app’s storage is removed. Device backups can have separate retention. You can remove task and journal content in the app and manage microphone, speech and notification access through Apple settings. For a question about data sent directly to support, email the contact above.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message. Share only what is necessary. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process request/network logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. The site stores your chosen language locally in your browser; it has no added advertising or analytics script.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information you directly supplied to support. Where applicable, rights may include access, restriction, objection and portability, and you may contact a relevant data-protection authority. Requests about Apple or RevenueCat-held data may require contacting those providers.

Updates

This notice will be updated if the released app’s data handling changes. Provider information: Apple privacy policy and RevenueCat privacy policy are linked below.

Your daily record

Tasks, completion history, your written or transcribed words and selected journal photos are linked to their date. Journal and My Days use the same day record. The diary summary uses completed tasks; it does not invent stories or feelings. Decorative Editorial photos and font preferences are stored separately from journal photos. This website does not receive your tasks, diary or photos. No separate audio archive is saved.
