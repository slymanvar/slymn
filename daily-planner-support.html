Support

· 2026-10-03

Contact

For questions, feedback or an issue, contact SLYMN Support.

What to include

Include your app version/build, iOS version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. Send a screenshot only if it contains no private journal content, identifiers or payment details.

Reminders

Check notification permission and Focus settings in iOS. Review the task date and reminder time. Changing a task should also update its reminder.

Voice entry

Check microphone and speech-recognition permissions. Device language, network and Apple service availability can affect transcription. You can use typed task entry instead.

Purchases

After release, use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple account used to buy Pro. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple account settings. Billing and refund requests are handled by Apple.

Personal content

Do not uninstall the app as a first troubleshooting step if it contains important local records. iCloud sync is not available in the current build. Ask support before taking steps that could remove your data.

Privacy requests

To ask about information directly sent to support, contact the support link above. Never email full journal archives or sensitive identity documents as part of an ordinary support request.

My Days and Journal

My Days shows dated task history. Journal opens your prepared diary page with your words, photos and completed-task summary. Swipe between diary pages or use the arrows; use the date button to jump to a day. Add or edit your story on that page, by typing or speaking. You do not need to write completed tasks again.

Appearance

In More → Appearance → Font, choose Handwriting, System or Rounded. In Storybook Today, scroll the task list while the fox scene stays visible. With Pro, tap an Editorial decorative photo to replace it, adjust its framing or restore the default. These photos are separate from diary photos. If Pro ends, your chosen photos are kept while the defaults are displayed.
