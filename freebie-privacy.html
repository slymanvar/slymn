Privacy Policy

Updated · 2026-10-04

Publisher and scope

K-POP FREEBIE is developed by Süleyman Varol under SLYMN. Contact: smanvar4255@gmail.com. This draft describes the agreed product design; final service configuration will be verified before release.

Your designs and photos

The planned editor stores designs, imported photos, drawings and preferences on your device. No app account is required. Optional photo access is limited to items you choose; exporting may request permission to save to Photos.

Optional AI suggestions

AI is not connected in the current development version. When enabled, only after you request it and accept the information shown in the app, a smaller photo copy and relevant design/request information will pass through the app’s server to OpenAI. The original photo is preserved. Normal editing does not require AI.

Purchases and quota

Apple handles payments. RevenueCat is planned for subscription verification; identifiers, transactions and entitlements may be processed. The AI server will validate access and quota. The developer does not receive your complete payment-card details.

Retention and removal

Remove local designs and items in the app, including Recently Deleted where available. Device backups have separate rules. Trial-use and purchase records may remain to prevent free-use resets. Exact server retention, hosting and transfer safeguards will be stated before AI is enabled.

Support and this website

Email support receives your address and message to answer your request. Do not send API keys or unnecessary private photos. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process request logs. These pages store your language choice locally and add no advertising or analytics script.

Your choices and rights

Manage permissions in iOS Settings. For information sent directly to support, contact the publisher about access, correction or deletion. Other applicable rights can include restriction, objection and portability; you may complain to a competent data-protection authority. Provider-held records may require a provider request.
