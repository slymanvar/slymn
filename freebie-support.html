Contact

Send questions, feedback or a problem report to smanvar4255@gmail.com.

Reporting a problem

Include app/build version, iOS version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. Remove private content from screenshots. Never email passwords, API keys or full payment details.

Protect your designs

Export important work before troubleshooting. Do not uninstall as a first step when local designs matter. Check Recently Deleted for removed designs. Cloud backup or sync is not promised by this preview.

Printing and QR codes

Check the paper size, orientation and print scaling. Choose whether to rotate, resize or split a design that does not fit. Print a test page for front/back alignment. Keep QR contrast and its clear surrounding space.

AI suggestions

AI is not connected yet. Normal editing, drawing, cutout and QR tools do not use AI credits. When available, AI suggestions will require an active Pro plan and a network connection.

Purchases

Purchases are not enabled in this development preview. After release, Restore Purchases should use the Apple account that purchased Pro. Billing, cancellation and refunds are handled through Apple.
