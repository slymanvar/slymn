Can I download SVAR now?

SVAR is in development. An App Store link will be added here when available.

Does the digital scale measure exact weight?

Do not treat photo or LiDAR analysis as an exact weighing scale. Device support and the actual measurement method will be explained in the app.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. Identification and numerical results can be wrong. Use them as a starting point and verify important decisions with a qualified specialist.

How can I report a problem?

Email the app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. Remove private details from screenshots.
