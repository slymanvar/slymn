Privacy Policy

Updated 2 October 2026

Publisher and local processing

SVRL is published by Süleyman Varol under SLYMN. No SVRL account is required. Documents, selected photos and OCR text are processed on the device using Apple's frameworks. The app has no document-upload server, third-party AI, advertising or analytics SDK. Local files include originals, crops, page order, OCR text and PDF/JPEG exports; a local database stores export metadata.

Permissions and sharing

Camera access is used for scanning. Photo and file pickers provide only your selected items. Adding to Photos is requested when you save there. Change permissions in iOS Settings. Sharing, saving outside the app and printing transfer your chosen document to your selected destination, whose practices then apply.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a draft page removes its original image. Discarding a draft deletes its folder; launch cleanup removes other old drafts. Deleting a visible recent export removes its files and history. Currently only the latest eight exports appear on the home screen; older exports remain and cannot be individually deleted there. iOS Delete App removes app-container data; offloading may retain it. Import copies are deleted after processing; temporary sharing copies on the next launch. Manage external copies separately.

Backups and free-use record

App files are not excluded from iOS backups and may be in iCloud or computer backups according to your settings. App deletion does not delete those backups. The Keychain stores a free-export counter and random document/page identifiers, without document contents. It is designed to persist and may survive reinstalling. No reset control is currently provided, and we cannot remotely delete local records.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple StoreKit 2 obtains products, processes purchases and checks entitlements through Apple's services. SVRL does not receive card/bank details and has no RevenueCat or purchase server. Local error logs exclude document contents, OCR text and file names; the app does not upload them. Development builds may record local timing. Apple's separate diagnostic sharing depends on your iOS preferences.

Website, support and rights

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Our code uses no advertising/analytics scripts or cookies; a language preference can be saved locally in your browser. GitHub may process hosting requests. GitHub feedback is public only if you submit it. Email support uses Gmail: we receive your address, message and chosen attachments to handle your enquiry, retained as needed for follow-up or legal requirements. Depending on applicable law, request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, or complain to a data protection authority. We cannot access your local documents remotely. Contact the publisher at the email below.
