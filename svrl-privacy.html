Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current SVRL PDF Scanner application and this website. Publisher and privacy contact: Süleyman Varol, publishing under SLYMN. Contact: smanvar4255@gmail.com.

1. Documents stay under your control

SVRL processes your selected documents and images on your device. Scanning, edge detection, crop and perspective correction, image enhancement, OCR and PDF creation use Apple's frameworks. SVRL has no document-upload server, third-party AI service or third-party SDK. Its code does not transmit your document contents or recognised text to us. No SVRL account is required.

Document contents can include personal information. SVRL processes that content to carry out the document tasks you request. Local storage is not the same as collection by the publisher.

2. Camera, Photos and Files

Camera access is requested for scanning. Live camera frames are processed in memory; captured pages are saved as local drafts. The system photo picker gives SVRL access to the items you select, without requesting access to your entire photo library. The system file picker gives access to selected files. Permission to add to Photos is requested only when you choose to save an image there. You can change camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings.

3. OCR and local storage

Text recognition uses Apple Vision; Live Text previews use VisionKit. Recognised text is stored with the local document draft and can be included as an invisible searchable text layer in the exported PDF.

The app stores original page images, document names, page order, crop and rotation settings and OCR text in its private Application Support area. Finished PDF or JPEG exports are stored there too. A local database records export names, dates, page counts, formats and file names. Preferences are stored locally in UserDefaults.

SVRL uses iOS file protection that protects files until the first device unlock after a restart. This does not constitute a separate encrypted cloud service.

4. Retention and deletion

Deleting a page from a draft also deletes its original image from the draft's storage. Discarding a draft deletes its folder. On launch, draft folders other than the current draft are cleaned up. Deleting an export available in Recent PDFs deletes its stored files and history record.

Current limitation: the home screen shows only the eight most recent exports. Older exports remain in the local files and database, have no automatic expiry and cannot currently be individually deleted through that screen. Deleting the app using iOS Delete App removes its app-container data; offloading the app may retain it. Export or back up anything you wish to keep before deleting the app.

Temporary sharing and printing copies are cleaned up on the next app launch. Temporary copies made while importing a PDF are deleted after the import operation finishes. Copies you save elsewhere, share with another app or print are outside SVRL's control and must be managed at their destination.

5. iOS backups

SVRL does not implement its own iCloud or other cloud synchronisation. Its Application Support files are not excluded from system backups, so documents and app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups according to your iOS settings. Removing data from the app does not necessarily remove copies already present in a backup or export. Manage those copies through the relevant backup or storage service.

6. Free-use record

The app stores the number of free exports used and random document and page identifiers in the iOS Keychain to enforce the free-use allowance. This record contains no document images or recognised text, is not sent to us, and is designed to persist. Keychain entries may remain after uninstalling and reinstalling the app, so reinstalling does not necessarily reset the free allowance. The app currently provides no control to reset this record, and we cannot remotely delete a record on your device.

7. Purchases through Apple

SVRL uses Apple StoreKit 2 to obtain product information, purchase and restore subscriptions and check verified purchase entitlements. StoreKit communicates with Apple's App Store services. SVRL has no purchase server and does not use RevenueCat or send an app account token. We do not receive your card or bank details from the app. Apple handles payment and account information under its privacy policy.

8. Diagnostics and tracking

SVRL includes no advertising, tracking, analytics or third-party crash-reporting tools. Limited local system error logs do not include document contents, OCR text or file names, and the app does not upload those logs. Development builds may store local launch timing information. Apple may separately provide diagnostic information according to your iOS analytics-sharing settings; that is an Apple service, not a SVRL tracking system.

9. Sharing you choose

Data leaves the app when you choose a destination in the share sheet, save a file to a location, save to Photos or print. The selected app, storage provider or printer then handles that data under its own practices. Review the destination before sharing sensitive documents.

10. Website, feedback and support

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The SVRL page embeds its own images and video and uses no advertising or analytics scripts; its own code does not set cookies. GitHub may process hosting request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

The community form opens a GitHub draft. It does not submit a message to us by itself. If you publish the draft on GitHub, its contents and your GitHub identity are public. Do not include private documents or identifiers. Use email for private support.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to handle your enquiry, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support or, where applicable, taking requested steps or fulfilling a contract. Email is handled through Gmail. We retain correspondence while needed to resolve and follow up the enquiry, or where legally required. We do not use it for advertising. Avoid sending sensitive document contents unless necessary.

11. Your choices and rights

You can revoke device permissions, manage local documents, change backup and diagnostic-sharing settings and manage Apple subscriptions. For information we hold through support correspondence, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or applicable portability, or to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it. Rights may be subject to applicable legal limitations. You may also complain to a competent data protection authority. We cannot access your local documents to fulfil a remote deletion request.

12. Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the application's data practices change. The update date appears above. For privacy questions, contact Süleyman Varol at smanvar4255@gmail.com.
