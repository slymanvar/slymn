Use & subscription information

Pre-release information. SVRL is not currently offered for purchase on this website. Final application terms will be completed before release and linked in the app.

Intended use

SVRL PDF Scanner is intended for document capture, crop correction, page organisation, OCR and PDF creation. Use only material you have permission to process. Check the finished PDF and recognised text before relying on them; scan and OCR results can contain errors.

Free use and planned Pro pricing

The planned offer is one complete free use, followed by Pro for continued use. The planned US subscription prices are $3.99 per week or $39.99 per year. Final products, local currency prices and availability will be displayed inside the app before an Apple purchase. This website cannot start a subscription or take payment.

Apple subscriptions

Paid subscriptions will be purchased through Apple and renew automatically unless cancelled. Manage or cancel them in your Apple account settings. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription. The app must provide a way to restore eligible purchases. Refund requests are handled through Apple's refund service under Apple's applicable rules.

Licence

The intended app licence is Apple's Standard End User Licence Agreement, unless a different licence is explicitly provided at release. Mandatory consumer rights remain applicable.

Community contributions

Submit only feedback you have the right to share. Public reviews, questions and feature requests appear through GitHub. Suggestions do not guarantee that a feature will be implemented or that an update will arrive on a particular date.

Before release

Publisher and contact details, final Pro entitlements, purchase disclosures and the application privacy policy must be completed against the released app. See privacy information and support.
